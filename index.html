📚 Programs

The Right Program for Every Little One

From tiny infants to ready-for-kindergarten explorers, we have a nurturing path tailored to every age and stage.

👶
Ages 6 months – 18 months

Infant Care

Our warm, attachment-based infant program provides the gentle, responsive care every baby deserves. Your little one gets 1:1 attention, sensory exploration, and routines tailored to their needs.

  • 1:4 infant-to-caregiver ratio
  • Individualized feeding & sleep schedules
  • Daily sensory play & tummy time
  • Daily digital photo updates to parents
  • On-site nursing room
  • Organic meals & snacks provided
🦒
Ages 18 months – 3 years

Toddler Adventures

Toddlers are natural explorers! Our program channels their boundless energy into purposeful play, language development, and early social skills through art, music, and outdoor discovery.

  • 1:6 toddler-to-caregiver ratio
  • Language-rich environment & circle time
  • Sensory bins, water & sand play
  • Outdoor play twice daily
  • STEM toys & building blocks
  • Potty training support
🎒
Ages 3 – 5 years

Preschool Prep

A structured yet joyful pre-K curriculum preparing children for kindergarten — covering early literacy, numeracy, science, social-emotional skills, and creative expression.

  • 1:8 ratio with lead & assistant teacher
  • Emergent literacy & pre-math skills
  • Garden science & nature journals
  • Kindergarten readiness assessments
  • Social-emotional learning curriculum
🌟
Ages 5 – 12 years

After-School Club

A homework-friendly, fun-filled afternoon program for school-age kids with enrichment activities, supervised homework time, and free play in a safe environment.

  • Supervised homework & reading time
  • Outdoor sports & games
  • Flexible drop-in days available

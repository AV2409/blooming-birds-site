A Family-Owned Daycare Rooted in Love & Learning

Founded in 2025 by Kritika and Akash Vij, Blooming Birds began with a simple dream: to create the kind of daycare they wished had existed for their own children—warm, joyful, nurturing, and deeply educational.

Every day at Blooming Birds is an opportunity to learn, explore, grow, and discover. And every day, we have the joy of watching little wings take flight.
